# Where the money moved — treatment

A 43-second data film on `/film/`, drawn in code by `static/film.js` from the data `build.py` bakes into the page.
Directed with the Lemo-Opuscar method (github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar, MIT), Data Storytelling grammar,
made our own: the site's paper, ink and current-teal instead of cream paper and a red–blue pencil.

## Structures considered

1. **A ranked race**: countries climb and overtake; one bar is the character. Clear, but stays at country level.
2. **Before → after**: Bangkok and the east corridor as a slope chart. Strong contrast, no sense of scale.
3. **Question first, a bar that opens into a map, a map that becomes a ranking** (chosen): starts from the regional
   picture, turns on the home country's surprising last place, and lets the native move (map → ranking) carry the
   reveal that the national average hides where money goes.

## Logline and arc

Thailand ranks last of eleven on momentum, yet inside it money is moving east.
Setup (the question, eleven bars) → turn (Thailand lands last after a silence; "averages hide where money goes")
→ inside (Bangkok's closed branches against the province that added the most formal jobs)
→ reveal (77 provinces lift off the map into a ranking) → ending (one district, close).

Every subject is chosen by rule, not by name: the home country's rank, Bangkok's branch change, the province with the
largest formal-job gain, the district first on money momentum. A new month can change who appears, never what is true.
